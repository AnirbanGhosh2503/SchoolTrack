## 1. Introduction

Stakeholder analysis is the process of identifying individuals, groups and organisations that can influence, participate in, or be affected by a project.

The purpose of this document is to identify the stakeholders associated with SchoolTrack, understand their interests and expectations, assess their potential influence and establish appropriate engagement strategies.

The findings will contribute to requirements engineering, system design and project governance.

## 4. Stakeholder Expectations

### 4.1 School Management
- Independent access to institutional data.
- Reduced dependency on external service providers.
- Reliable attendance and transportation monitoring.
- Convenient reporting and administrative oversight.
- Appropriate data security and institutional control.

### 4.2 School Administration
- Easy student registration and record management.
- Convenient retrieval of historical attendance information.
- Searchable and downloadable reports.
- Reliable identification of student movement events.

### 4.3 IT/Computer Department
- Secure and manageable system architecture.
- Administrative control over users and devices.
- Database backup and recovery facilities.
- Maintainability and technical documentation.

### 4.4 Teachers
- Access to relevant student information.
- Convenient attendance-related information.
- Appropriate restrictions on access to confidential records.

### 4.5 Transport Department
- Bus-wise student movement records.
- Boarding and deboarding information.
- Route and trip management.
- Identification of missing or incomplete movement events.

### 4.6 Bus Operators
- Simple device operation.
- Reliable recording of RFID scans.
- Minimal manual intervention.
- Clear indication of successful and unsuccessful scans.

### 4.7 Students and Parents/Guardians
- Reliable student identification.
- Protection of personal information.
- Accurate recording of movement events.
- Appropriate access to relevant information, subject to institutional policy.

### 4.8 Development Team
- Clearly defined institutional requirements.
- Access to relevant technical specifications.
- Availability of testing resources.
- Timely feedback and evaluation.
- Appropriate development and deployment resources.

### 4.9 External Technology Providers
- Provision of relevant hardware specifications, infrastructure or services.
- Technical compatibility information.
- Support for procurement and integration where required.

## 7. Stakeholder-Related Risks

Potential stakeholder-related risks include:

1. Incomplete or unclear institutional requirements.
2. Conflicting expectations between administrative and transport departments.
3. Limited availability of institutional personnel for consultations.
4. Resistance to changes in existing operational procedures.
5. Delays in obtaining hardware specifications from external providers.
6. Unclear responsibility for long-term system maintenance.
7. Uncertainty regarding the level of access granted to different user groups.

Appropriate mitigation strategies will be incorporated into the project Risk Register.

## 8. Open Questions for Stakeholder Consultation

The following matters require further investigation:

1. Who will formally sponsor and approve the project?
2. Who will have complete administrative control over the application?
3. Will teachers require individual login accounts?
4. Should parents or guardians have access to movement notifications?
5. Who will manage student registration and RFID card assignments?
6. Who will be responsible for bus and route configuration?
7. Who will handle technical maintenance after deployment?
8. What information should each stakeholder group be permitted to access?
9. Will the institution permit cloud hosting and third-party infrastructure services?
10. Will the institution provide access to existing hardware specifications and documentation?

## 9. Conclusion

The stakeholder analysis identifies the primary individuals and groups associated with the proposed SchoolTrack system.

Understanding their expectations, influence and operational requirements will help ensure that the system is designed to address actual institutional needs.

The stakeholder register, power-interest classification and engagement strategy will be reviewed and updated as further information becomes available.
