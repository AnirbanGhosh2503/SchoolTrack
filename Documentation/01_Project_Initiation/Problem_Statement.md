# Problem Statement

## 1. Background

Educational institutions increasingly rely on digital systems to manage student information, attendance and transportation-related activities. RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) technology provides a convenient method of identifying students and recording their movements through designated checkpoints.

The institution currently employs an RFID-based system for monitoring student movements, including entry into and exit from the school premises, as well as boarding and deboarding of school buses.

## 2. Existing Problem

The existing RFID infrastructure and its associated data management services are provided and managed by an external service provider.

However, the institution currently faces limitations in independently accessing and managing the data generated through this system. This dependency restricts the institution's ability to exercise complete control over its student movement records, access historical information independently and customise data management according to its administrative requirements.

Consequently, the institution seeks an alternative solution that provides greater autonomy, transparency and control over its RFID-generated information.

## 3. Identified Challenges

The primary challenges identified are:

1. **Limited Data Accessibility:** The institution cannot independently access all the data generated by its existing RFID infrastructure.
2. **External Dependency:** The current arrangement relies on an external service provider for data management and accessibility.
3. **Centralised Data Control:** The institution lacks complete independent control over the storage, retrieval and management of its movement records.
4. **Reporting Limitations:** There is a need for an accessible mechanism to retrieve, analyse and export student movement information.
5. **System Flexibility:** The institution requires a solution that can be adapted to its administrative and operational requirements.

## 4. Proposed Solution

The proposed project aims to develop an institution-controlled RFID-based Student Movement Management System integrating dedicated hardware components with a centralised software platform.

The system will facilitate the collection, transmission, storage, processing and retrieval of RFID-generated student movement records.

A web-based administrative dashboard is proposed to provide authorised personnel with access to relevant student information, attendance records, transportation activities and analytical reports.

The system will also aim to provide data export facilities in commonly used formats, including CSV, Excel and PDF.

The initial target population is approximately 2,000 students.

## 5. Expected Outcome

The intended outcome is an integrated hardware-software solution that enables the institution to independently manage its RFID-generated student movement data, reduce dependency on external data management services and improve the accessibility and efficiency of its administrative operations.

The final implementation and deployment architecture will be determined following detailed stakeholder consultation, hardware assessment and technical feasibility analysis.
